# Access certification procedure

**Scope:** Four security groups, membership snapshot, reviewer decisions, and exception remediation.

1. Identify each group's business owner and documented access criteria.
2. Review account status, department, job role, privileged rights, and exceptions.
3. Record Approve, Revoke, or Investigate with reviewer, date, and reason.
4. Obtain business-owner approval before changes outside a controlled lab test.
5. Preserve original snapshot and decision record. Track ticket/change IDs.
6. Revoke approved excess permissions, validate AD state, and retest effective access after new logon where applicable.
7. Archive evidence securely and schedule periodic recertification.

**Lab outcome:** One deliberate excess Finance membership was revoked; correct IT membership retained. Seven other membership entries were pending, not certified.
