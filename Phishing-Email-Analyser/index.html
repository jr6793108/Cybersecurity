    Security and Privacy Disclaimer

    For educational and informational use only. This analyser uses rule-based checks to highlight possible phishing warning signs. Its 0–100 score is not a validated probability of phishing, and no result guarantees that an email is safe or malicious. A Low Risk result does not establish legitimacy.

    The tool reviews pasted text only. It does not open or validate links, scan attachments, independently authenticate senders, or verify SPF, DKIM, or DMARC with mail servers. It may miss sophisticated attacks or flag legitimate messages.

    Privacy: Analysis runs locally in your browser. The application does not intentionally upload pasted email content, use tracking, or contact external APIs. Your browser, extensions, and the static hosting provider may have separate behavior, including ordinary site-access logs. Avoid pasting passwords, verification codes, personal data, or confidential business information.

    If an email seems suspicious, do not follow its links or open its attachments. Verify requests through a trusted, independently obtained contact method and report suspected phishing through your organization or email provider.
